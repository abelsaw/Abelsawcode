# Weekly summary — source catalog (Tech & AI only)

This is the curated set of credible sources the `weekly-summary` skill searches
each Friday across **AI Models & Research, Infrastructure & Compute, and
Enterprise Tech & Security** — the three lenses the Chief Technology Officer
this skill writes for cares about.

The skill ranks stories by **cross-citation count** across these sources.

**Out of scope (do not include):** HR / workforce / talent / layoffs /
org-design / culture / DEI / benefits / compensation stories. Even when a tech
company runs a layoff cycle, only the tech-strategy shift may be included —
never the workforce angle on its own.

## Cross-citation rule

A story qualifies for the ranked top 10 when it meets BOTH:

1. It is cited by **≥2 catalog sources** within the resolved Mon-Fri (or
   user-specified) window.
2. **At least one of those citations** comes from the **Tier-1 group below**
   (global tier-1 news OR an AI lab OR a big-tech newsroom OR a tech-research
   firm's own publication). IT specialty trade outlets and finance trade press
   can supply the second citation but cannot alone clear the bar.

If you cannot meet that combination for 10 stories, fill the rest of the top 10
with the next-strongest candidates and **flag the thin entries** in the brief.

## Do not count — excluded sources

These sources may appear in WebSearch results but **must not contribute** to the
cross-citation count. They tend to publish hype/aggregation rather than
original reporting:

- MEXC News and other crypto-exchange newsrooms
- OneKey Blog, cryptobriefing.com, generic crypto / token-economy blogs
- Memeburn
- IBTimes (low signal-to-noise; cite only the underlying primary source)
- Slashdot (syndication-only; trace to original)
- Tom's Hardware **when syndicating** (only count when original reporting)
- Yahoo Finance (count once for the underlying wire; do not also count Yahoo separately)
- Aggregator / SEO-spam AI blogs: mean.ceo, llm-stats.com, releasebot.io, ai2.work, finalroundai.com, AICC, dentro.de/ai roundups, originbrief, buildfastwithai
- Mid-tier finance content farms: insidermonkey, marketscreener, stocktitan, gurufocus (when only summarizing another source)
- LinkedIn posts, X/Twitter threads, YouTube videos (use as leads, never as the citation)
- Personal blogs, Substack opinion posts (unless from a recognized analyst — see Tier-1 thought leadership)

When one of these appears in search results, **trace to the primary source** in
the catalog above and cite that one instead.

## Out-of-scope sources (HR / workforce — removed by design)

This skill is **pure technology and AI**. The following categories of source
that the prior catalog included have been **intentionally removed** and must
not be re-added unless the skill scope is broadened by the user:

- HR-specialty trade press (SHRM, HR Dive, HR Executive, HR Brew, Personnel Today, People Matters, HR Asia, ETHRWorld, AHRI, HKIHRM, HR Reporter)
- HR-specialty consultancies (Mercer, WTW, Aon, Gallup, Korn Ferry)
- Layoff trackers (layoffhedge, layoffs.fyi, trueup, skillsyncer, layoffalert)

If you find a tech/AI story whose only sources are HR outlets or layoff
trackers, **drop it** — it does not belong in this skill's scope.

## Discovery rules

The skill MAY add a new source to **Auto-discovered sources** below when ALL of
the following are true:

1. The source published the story in question within the resolved window.
2. The source is a recognized tech firm, lab, university, government body, or
   tier-1 publication — not on the exclusion list above, and not an HR /
   workforce outlet.
3. The story is independently cross-cited by at least one Tier-1 catalog
   source already (the new source is corroborating, not the sole citation).
4. The source is reachable via WebFetch (so future runs can verify).

When you add a discovered source, mention it in the final report to the user.
