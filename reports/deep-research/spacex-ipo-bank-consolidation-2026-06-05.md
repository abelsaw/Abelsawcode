## TL;DR

SpaceX (codename: **Project Apex**) is mid-roadshow for what would be the **largest IPO in history**:
- **Target valuation:** $1.77 trillion at $135/share
- **Raise:** ~$75 billion (555.6 million shares)
- **Listing:** Nasdaq, ticker **SPCX**, planned **June 12, 2026**; pricing June 11
- **Lead-left:** Goldman Sachs · **Lead underwriters:** Goldman Sachs + Morgan Stanley · **Bookrunners:** BofA, Citi, JPMorgan · **Total syndicate:** ~21-25 banks · **Underwriting fees:** ~$500M
- **Bank view divergence is sharp:** Goldman (lead-left) projects SpaceX AI revenue rising 100x by 2030; Morningstar DCF lands at $780B (~48% below market); Cathie Wood / ARK base case $2.5T by 2030

This is one of three consecutive mega-IPOs from private-tech giants in 2026 — alongside **Anthropic** (confidential S-1 filed early June 2026 at $965B post-money) and **OpenAI** (confidential filing in progress at $852B reference).

## 3. Deal-structure speculation

### Confirmed
- **Parent SpaceX is the listing vehicle** (not Starlink) — confirmed across Bloomberg, CNBC, Investing.com, Hargreaves Lansdown
- Class B super-voting shares: Musk retains ~85% voting power post-IPO; can elect all board members; only he can fire himself as CEO [search-only]
- **China exclusion:** SpaceX barred Hong Kong and Mainland China investors from the $75B offering — BigGo Finance [search-only]

### Earlier expectations vs. actual structure
- **Pre-2026 consensus:** Most sell-side analysts expected SpaceX would keep parent private and IPO Starlink as a "utility" subsidiary while parent remained an "R&D" entity (Phemex Academy, IG International, multiple analyst notes pre-2026) [search-only]
- **Why structure shifted to parent listing:** No single source nails this; implied reasoning is (a) Musk's December endorsement reset planning, (b) $75B raise size requires the parent valuation pool, (c) xAI merger (Feb 2026) complicated separate Starlink listing
- **Post-IPO Starlink spin-off remains in play:** Analysts still model a 2027 spin-off as the "phase 2"

### xAI merger background (Feb 2026)
- xAI was merged into SpaceX in February 2026 — this is a material pre-IPO event
- ION Analytics ECM Pulse: xAI merger introduced ~$9B in related-party debt risk + raised governance concerns about non-arm's-length transactions

## 5. Risks / concerns banks are flagging

### Governance (largest concern by frequency)
- **Class B super-voting shares**: Musk = ~85% voting power post-IPO — multiple sources
- **Key-person risk amplified by charter language** that renounces expectation Musk will prioritize SpaceX opportunities first — Whalesbook, MarketWise [search-only]
- **Institutional pushback already public:**
  - **Denmark's AkademikerPension** blacklisted SpaceX citing "catastrophic governance structure" [search-only]
  - **CalPERS CEO + NYC Comptroller Mark Levine + NY State Comptroller Thomas DiNapoli** signed joint letter on "extreme governance structure" [search-only]

### Cash burn
- $4.9B operating loss in 2025 [search-only]
- xAI cash spend doubling+ in 2026 (per above)
- "$75B IPO proceeds could be burnt through quickly; IPO may be first raise in a perennial cycle of equity raising and burn" — ION Analytics [search-only]

### Related-party debt
- **~$9B in related-party debt** from AI equipment leases revealed in S-1 — Whalesbook, ION Analytics [search-only]
- February 2026 **xAI merger not conducted at arm's length** (Musk owns both sides) — flagged by multiple analyst critiques

### Valuation math
- At $1.75-2T: "unfathomably expensive by any reasonable valuation ratios — P/E, P/B, P/S" — Whalesbook synthesis of analyst critiques [search-only]
- Fortune calculation: SpaceX needs to grow 600x in a decade to justify $1.75T — "no company has ever come close"

### China exclusion / market access
- SpaceX explicitly barred Hong Kong and Mainland China investors — limits secondary demand and signals Musk's geopolitical positioning [search-only]
