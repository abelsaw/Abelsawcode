# SpaceX IPO — Underwriter Bank Stories & Growth Forecasts

Deep-research consolidation, 2026-06-06. Question: "find all the stories by these banks on
SpaceX's IPO and growth forecast" (banks = the SpaceX IPO underwriters: Goldman Sachs, Morgan
Stanley, Bank of America, Citi, JPMorgan).

Method note: WebFetch was blocked (HTTP 403) across all targets in this environment, so every
figure below is **[search-only]** — corroborated across 3+ independent outlets (many citing WSJ,
Bloomberg, Reuters roadshow reporting) rather than read from the primary research PDFs. Bank
"forecasts" are roadshow projections reported by the press, not publicly posted equity research.

---

## 1. The deal & the syndicate (confidence: HIGH)

- IPO codename **"Project Apex"**; expected June 2026 on Nasdaq (ticker SPCX). S-1 filed ~May 20.
- Terms: **$135/share, ~555.6M shares, ~$75B raise** at a **~$1.75T valuation** — the largest IPO
  in history. Underwriting fees reported ~$500M.
- **21-bank syndicate** ("one of the largest assembled in recent years"); 16 in smaller roles.
- **Active bookrunners / lead underwriters:**
  - **Goldman Sachs** — lead-left (most senior slot: allocation, pricing, institutional outreach).
  - **Morgan Stanley** — co-lead.
  - **Bank of America, Citigroup, JPMorgan** — active bookrunners (listed alphabetically on the
    preliminary prospectus alongside GS and MS).

## 2. Goldman Sachs forecast (lead-left) (confidence: HIGH — internally consistent)

Goldman's roadshow model (2030, vs 2025 base):
- **Total revenue: $474B by 2030** (from $18.7B in 2025).
  - AI division: **$322B** (~100x from ~$3.2B in 2025; +388% to $15.6B in 2026; $34.5B in 2027).
  - Starlink: **$144B**.
  - Launch/rocket: **$8.3B** (from $4.1B in 2025).
  - (322 + 144 + 8.3 = 474.3 — internally consistent.)
- **Adjusted EBITDA: $352B by 2030** (from $6.6B in 2025; ~52x).
- Goldman framed this as implying an IPO valuation of ~$1.78T.

## 3. Morgan Stanley forecast (co-lead; Adam Jonas) (confidence: HIGH)

- Headline: **$3.4T revenue by 2040** (~182-fold), adjusted **EBITDA ~$2.7T** (WSJ-reported).
- 2030: total revenue **~$330B**, AI **~$190B**, adjusted EBITDA **~$230B**.
- **Adam Jonas:** "Starlink alone would justify a **$500B** valuation as a standalone business";
  bull case rests on three pillars — Starlink, Starship, and AI ventures.
- Frames the global space economy growing from **$469B (2025) to $1.1T (2040)**.
- Note the GS vs MS gap on 2030 total revenue: **$474B (GS) vs $330B (MS)** — same direction,
  materially different magnitude.

## 4. JPMorgan, Bank of America, Citi (confidence: HIGH on roles)

- **JPMorgan** — most visible distribution push: CEO **Jamie Dimon** pitching the IPO to **2,500+
  high-net-worth clients**, simulcast to ~**90 locations across 26 states**, alongside Mary Callahan
  Erdoes and SpaceX's **Gwynne Shotwell (President)** and **Bret Johnsen (CFO)**.
- **Bank of America & Citi** — active bookrunners; no distinct published growth forecast surfaced
  (their named contribution is bookrunning/distribution, not separate revenue models). [search-only;
  absence of a standalone BofA/Citi forecast is "not found," not "confirmed none exists."]

## 5. The counter-narrative — the bear case (confidence: HIGH)

- **Morningstar** fair value: **~$780B** — roughly **48% below the ~$1.5T private mark** and ~55%
  below the $1.75T IPO target. Advice: "smart investors will wait" for a post-lockup pullback.
- Valuation math: at $18.7B 2025 revenue, $1.75T implies a **price-to-sales ~94x** (vs Nvidia ~21x).
- Key risks flagged: **AI-segment feasibility** (orbital data centers a "longshot"); **governance**
  — Musk's expected **~85% voting control** via dual-class shares; **related-party xAI merger**.
- Bull data point even skeptics concede: SpaceX did **~90% of payload mass to orbit in 2025** across
  167 launches; Falcon 9 cost/kg driven below ~$1,500 (>95% vs legacy benchmarks).

---

## Bottom line

The underwriters are selling a convergence story — Starlink (cash engine), Starship (launch
monopoly), and AI/xAI (the upside multiplier). **Goldman** anchors on ~$474B revenue / $352B EBITDA
by 2030; **Morgan Stanley** stretches to **$3.4T by 2040** with Starlink alone "worth $500B";
**JPMorgan** owns the wealth-channel distribution. The single most important caveat for any reader:
the entire premium rides on the **AI segment** (the biggest 2030 line and the biggest uncertainty),
and **Morningstar values the company at roughly half** the IPO target.
