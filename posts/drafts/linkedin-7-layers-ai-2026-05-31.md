We keep talking about prompts. The real shift is underneath.

The cleanest mental model I've seen stacks AI in 7 layers:

Token. Prompt. Context. Agent. Harness. MCP. Skills.

Read it bottom to top and the story is obvious. We started by chasing better answers. We're ending with systems that coordinate work.

The prompt is just the doorway. Behind it: memory held as context, a harness keeping order, MCP wiring in external tools, and skills the agent calls on its own.

AI stopped being a question box. It became a manager.

Where does your stack sit today?
