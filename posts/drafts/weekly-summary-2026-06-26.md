Ten stories from Jun 22-26. The AI cost equation tightened across every lens. Custom chips, an infra roll-up, a 100,000-job restructure threat, mass layoffs at a hyperscaler, talent exodus — all the same direction. Serve tokens cheaper, run leaner, or get redeployed. Ranked by how loudly they were cited.

1. OpenAI and Broadcom unveiled Jalapeño on Tuesday — OpenAI's first custom chip, built for inference. Nine-month tape-out (fastest ASIC of this class ever), ~50% cheaper than typical AI GPUs. Gigawatt-scale deployment with Microsoft and other partners starts end-2026 and runs through 2029. The cost baseline just got reset.

2. Anthropic told Senators Scott and Warren that Alibaba/Qwen-linked actors ran ~25,000 fake accounts for 28.8 million Claude exchanges between April 22 and June 5 — targeting agentic reasoning, software engineering, long-horizon tasks. Congress is preparing sanctions on Chinese AI rivals.

3. Volkswagen is weighing up to 100,000 layoffs and shutting four German plants — Hanover, Zwickau, Emden, Audi Neckarsulm — directly affecting 45,000+ workers. Biggest restructuring in the company's 89-year history. The 19,000 cuts from the June 18 AGM now look like the floor.

4. Oracle's annual report Monday: 21,000 fewer staff over the year, 13% of headcount. Restructuring cost $1.8B versus $374M prior year. The filing named AI directly.

5. Qualcomm bought Modular for $3.92 billion in stock. Modular's software runs AI models across any chip — the cleanest CUDA-alternative play anyone has built. Qualcomm is now in the AI infrastructure fight, not just at the edge.

6. Two more Gemini researchers — Jonas Adler (coding) and Alexander Pritzel (pretraining, AlphaFold co-author) — left Google for Anthropic. Four senior DeepMind exits in six days. Pre-IPO equity is beating Google's salary stack.

7. FedEx Q4: EPS $6.31 beat $6.02; revenue $25.0B beat by $730M; FY26 revenue $94.7B versus $87.9B prior. First print after the June 1 Freight spin-off — the slimmer FedEx works.

8. Carnival posted a record Q2 — adjusted EPS $0.41 beat by $0.08, revenue $6.7B, customer deposits at an all-time high of $9.0B. Discretionary spend on experiences is still expanding.

9. NVIDIA at ISC unveiled the BioNeMo Agent Toolkit, Halos for Robotics (first full-stack physical-AI safety system), and 35 new European AI supercomputers in development. The sovereign-infra count keeps building.

10. Enterprise AI buyers are tired of token-volume pitches and rotating to measured efficiency — CNBC's Friday read. The frontier-spend narrative is hitting a cost-discipline wall. Same wall Jalapeño is built to climb.

Carrying into next week: VW's 100K test case is the European AI-restructuring template, written live. Karp's "tokenmaxxing" call from two weeks ago is now a vendor-design constraint, a procurement criterion, and a chip-architecture choice — inside one week. Cost discipline is the story.
