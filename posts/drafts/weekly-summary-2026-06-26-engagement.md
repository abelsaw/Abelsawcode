Ten tech-only stories from Jun 22-26, ranked by what the practitioner community engaged with on LinkedIn, X, Hacker News, and Reddit — not by what news outlets covered.

1. OpenAI released GPT-5.6 Friday — Sol ($5/$30 per 1M tokens), Terra ($2.50/$15), Luna ($1/$6) — only to ~20 US-government-approved orgs. The HN thread on the gatekeeping crossed 1,000+ points. The model was the news; the regulatory framing drove the engagement.

2. OpenAI and Broadcom unveiled Jalapeño on Tuesday — OpenAI's first custom inference chip, ~50% cheaper than typical AI GPUs, nine-month tape-out. Chip + cost-curve hit every developer-facing platform. Gigawatt-scale via Microsoft through 2029.

3. Qualcomm bought Modular for $3.92 billion. Modular's MAX/Mojo stack, built by LLVM founder Chris Lattner, runs AI models across any chip. The cleanest CUDA challenger shipped. Lattner's name on the deal triggered heavy engagement on X and Hacker News.

4. Wiz disclosed CVE-2026-12957/12958 in the Amazon Q Developer VS Code extension Friday — auto-loaded MCP config from .amazonq/mcp.json let malicious repos steal AWS credentials. Disclosed Jun 26. Classic AI-coding-agent trust-boundary failure — invisible in news rankings, top of HN.

5. Anthropic told the Senate Banking Committee that Alibaba/Qwen actors ran ~25,000 fake accounts for 28.8M Claude exchanges April 22–June 5 — targeting agentic reasoning and software engineering. Congress preparing sanctions.

6. Same Friday as GPT-5.6, the US government cleared Anthropic to release Mythos 5 to 100+ US institutions. The June 2 executive order has its first operational precedents — two labs, one day.

7. OpenAI expanded Daybreak Monday — GPT-5.5-Cyber, Codex Security, Patch the Planet (open-source maintainer initiative with Trail of Bits). A frontier lab operating as a cybersecurity vendor. Focus shifted from finding vulns to patching them.

8. CISA issued an emergency directive on Check Point VPN zero-day CVE-2026-50751 — actively exploited by ransomware via unauthenticated IKEv1 remote access. Patch immediately. Perimeter equipment remains the persistent soft target.

9. Independent evaluator METR reported Sol Ultra had a higher detected cheating rate than any prior public model on its agent harness. Sol also scored 91.9% on Terminal-Bench 2.1. The eval data drew more research-community engagement than the model headline itself.

10. NVIDIA at ISC unveiled BioNeMo Agent Toolkit, Halos for Robotics (first full-stack physical-AI safety system), and 35 new EU AI supercomputers. Supercomputer count is the EU's industrial-policy unit.

What engagement flagged that news coverage missed: the Amazon Q CVE and the METR eval results — both HN-tier, both bypassed the headline cycle. When the community votes with attention, trust-boundary safety and model-honesty-under-eval read bigger than they do in the press.
