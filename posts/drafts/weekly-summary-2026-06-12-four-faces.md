Five trading days. Four stories. One thread.

2026 is the year AI moved from balance-sheet line to publicly priced asset — and this week the calendar caught up to the revenue line.

CAPITAL. SpaceX listed Friday on Nasdaq as SPCX. A $135 offer closed at $160.95 (+19%); about $75B raised, the largest IPO in history; first-day cap above $2T. Goldman beat Morgan Stanley, JPMorgan, Citi, BofA and UBS to land lead-left. The space-and-compute bet now has a public-market price every capex assumption re-anchors to.

CAPABILITY. Anthropic shipped Claude Fable 5 on Tuesday — its first generally available Mythos-class model. $10 input / $50 output per million tokens. 1M context, 128k output. Day-one on the Claude API, AWS, Bedrock, Vertex AI and Microsoft Foundry. Free on Pro, Max, Team and Enterprise through June 22. Two weeks after Anthropic confidentially filed for IPO and three weeks after Series H closed at $965B. The frontier tier now has a public price tag.

DISTRIBUTION. OpenAI confirmed a confidential S-1 on Monday. "We expect it to leak so we're just announcing it." $852B last private mark. Annualized revenue past $25B. With Anthropic filed June 1 and OpenAI confirmed June 8, the two-horse race now has parallel IPO calendars inside a seven-day window. A Sep-Nov 2026 listing window is legible.

DEMAND VERDICT. Palantir's Alex Karp went on CNBC Tuesday. "Every single enterprise we deal with is unhappy with the frontier labs." Called out "tokenmaxxing" — burning tokens to signal productivity instead of producing outcomes. Said most of Anthropic's public projects "run on Palantir." From a CEO whose Q1 US commercial grew 133% YoY and whose Rule of 40 hit 145% — the tier with Nvidia, Micron and SK hynix.

Four stories. Capital, Capability, Distribution, Demand verdict. Same news cycle. The supply side is being financed publicly at a pace capex roadmaps were not built for. The buyer's verdict has not caught up to the seller's valuation.

What I am carrying into next week: Karp's "unhappy" lands harder when SpaceX trades above $2T the same Friday. For a CTO whose remit spans strategy, HR and IT, the operational gap between capacity financed and outcomes delivered is where the next twelve months play out — at the procurement layer, at the org-design layer, and at the integration layer that decides whether the model becomes an outcome or just a token bill.
