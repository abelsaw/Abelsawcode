Five trading days. Four stories. One thread.

2026 is the year AI moved from balance-sheet line to publicly priced asset — and this week the calendar caught up to the revenue line.

CAPITAL. SpaceX listed Friday on Nasdaq as SPCX. A $135 offer closed at $160.95 (+19%); ~$75B raised on 555.6M shares, the largest IPO in history; first-day cap above $2T, pushing to ~$2.2T in after-hours. Goldman beat Morgan Stanley, JPMorgan, Citi, BofA and UBS for lead-left. The space-and-compute bet now has a public-market price every capex assumption re-anchors to.

CAPABILITY. Anthropic shipped Claude Fable 5 on Tuesday — its first generally available Mythos-class model. $10 input / $50 output per million tokens. 1M context, 128k output. Day-one on Anthropic, AWS Bedrock, Vertex AI and Microsoft Foundry. Free on Pro, Max, Team and Enterprise through June 22, then metered. Post-launch reviews now have it at 80.3% on SWE-Bench Pro — 11 points clear of the next-best model. The frontier tier now has a public price tag and a public benchmark.

DISTRIBUTION. OpenAI confirmed a confidential S-1 on Monday. "We expect it to leak so we're just announcing it." $852B last private mark; weekend reporting puts the target listing valuation closer to $1T, with Goldman, Morgan Stanley and JPMorgan circling as joint leads. TBPN and Tomoro acquired in the same window, plus a $4B-backed new subsidiary — the enterprise-and-brand surface area is being hardened in real time. With Anthropic filed June 1 and OpenAI confirmed June 8, the two-horse race now has parallel IPO calendars.

DEMAND VERDICT. Palantir's Alex Karp went on CNBC Tuesday. "Every single enterprise we deal with is unhappy with the frontier labs." Called out "tokenmaxxing" — burning tokens to signal productivity instead of producing outcomes. Said most of Anthropic's public projects "run on Palantir" — while also calling Anthropic the "leading frontier model company." From a CEO whose Q1 US commercial grew 133% YoY and whose Rule of 40 hit 145%.

Four stories. Capital, Capability, Distribution, Demand verdict. Same news cycle. The supply side is being financed publicly at a pace capex roadmaps were not built for. The buyer's verdict has not caught up to the seller's valuation.

What I am carrying into next week: Karp's "unhappy" lands harder when SpaceX trades above $2T the same Friday. For a CTO whose remit spans strategy, HR and IT, the operational gap between capacity financed and outcomes delivered is where the next twelve months play out — at procurement, at org design, and at the integration layer that decides whether the model becomes an outcome or just a token bill.
