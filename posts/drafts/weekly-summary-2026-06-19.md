Five stories from Monday — the only trading day inside this week so far. I am writing this as a partial-week landscape, with the full Friday wrap to come.

The week opened on geopolitics. A US-Iran MOU re-priced oil and equities inside one trading session. SpaceX kept climbing, three days in. The real decision lands Wednesday under a new Fed Chair. And the HR story is BBC's 2,000-job restructure.

1. The US and Iran signed a memorandum of understanding ending the 15-week conflict and reopening the Strait of Hormuz. Brent crude tumbled ~5% to a 3-month low near $83. The S&P 500 hit record highs (+1.9%), the Nasdaq +3.1%, Nikkei +5.5%, Kospi +5.7%. The geopolitical-risk premium just got removed in one session.

2. SpaceX SPCX jumped ~20% on its first full trading day to close at $192.50, with an intraday all-time high of $193. Market cap is now near $2.8 trillion — ahead of Amazon, Microsoft and Tesla on paper. Three sessions, +58% from the $135 IPO price.

3. The Federal Reserve meets Tuesday and Wednesday — Kevin Warsh's first meeting as Chair. Rates broadly expected to hold at 3.50-3.75%. The watch is the dot plot and whether the easing bias gets removed. May CPI came in at 4.2% YoY, the highest since April 2023 on the Iran-war energy spike — which today's MOU just relieved.

4. The BBC ordered every department to cut about 10% of costs, totaling ~2,000 job losses over two years and £500M in savings. The news division — TV, websites, radio, regional apps — is most affected. Ex-Google exec Matt Brittin is running the restructure after Tim Davie's May resignation. The "ex-platform-exec runs media restructure" pattern is repeating.

5. Palantir PLTR rebounded +5.25% on Monday to $134.71, riding the broader rally. Karp's "every enterprise is unhappy with the frontier labs" line from last week's CNBC interview is still active in coverage. The buyer-side narrative on the frontier labs is now an investable thesis.

What I am carrying into the rest of the week: an IT/AI-lens gap in the Mon landscape — all the major model and product beats from last week (Apple Siri-on-Gemini, Anthropic Fable 5, OpenAI S-1) have not had Mon Jun 15 follow-ons that cleared the catalog bar. That changes between now and Friday. Wednesday's FOMC is the week's real hinge. For a CTO whose remit spans strategy, HR and IT, the cost-of-capital read sets the size of every AI investment case for H2.
