---POST---
One-third of your people survived 15 major changes last year.

Then you asked them to get excited about the 16th.

Gartner 2026: 73% of HR leaders report change fatigue. The fix isn't a better rally. It's routinizing change - low-drama, embedded.

Stop launching change. Start absorbing it.
