Ten stories defined SEA's week. Royal pardons, joint newsroom probes, Michelin stars, dissolved assemblies, arrested senators, multinational scam-compound strikes. Ranked by how loudly the catalog cited them.

1. Thaksin Shinawatra received a royal pardon ending parole early and removing his travel ban. Bloomberg reported he is heading to Dubai. Thai politics' longest rehabilitation arc just closed.

2. Tempo, Kompas, Suara and Tribunnews jointly debunked the "foreign-backed" framing of Indonesia's 2025 protests. Amnesty's parallel report confirms the framing was military-coordinated. The press defended itself collectively. That is a structural moment.

3. The Michelin Guide named eleven Vietnamese restaurants with One Star across Hanoi, HCMC and Da Nang. First double-digit year in the Guide's Vietnam history. Vietnamese gastronomy entered a new tier of recognition.

4. Johor Assembly dissolved. Snap state election within 60 days. Barisan Nasional contests all 56 seats without Pakatan Harapan. Anwar's Unity Government faces its first real coalition test, with Negeri Sembilan signaling parallel snap polls.

5. Senator Jinggoy Estrada was arrested for alleged P573M plunder in the Philippine flood-control scandal. First sitting senator arrested in the broader case. Sandiganbayan moved while the Senate bloc battle continued.

6. Meta, Microsoft, Coinbase and Starlink coordinated with the US DOJ and Royal Thai Police to disrupt SEA scam compound networks. 63 arrests, 1.4 million Meta accounts disabled. The forced-labor compound system finally has multinational enforcement against it.

7. A Cayetano-bloc Senate hearing produced sworn testimony from 18 ex-Marines naming Marcos Jr., Sotto, Tulfo and de Lima as alleged recipients of suitcase cash. Interior Secretary Remulla confronted Senator Padilla on camera. Filipino civic politics entered viral-spectacle phase.

8. KPK arrested Indonesia's Deputy Immigration Minister Silmy Karim over an alleged Rp 145.5B extortion scheme targeting foreign residents. Prabowo dismissed him by decree. Anti-corruption posture, second this month.

9. Thailand's 1-trillion-baht Land Bridge was sent back for fresh Environmental and Health Impact Assessment after independent researchers found organism density 38x higher than the original study claimed. Fishing communities and the NHRC have been waiting for this opening.

10. At the ASEAN U19 in Medan, a Malaysian player kicked a Singaporean opponent in the chest. The Vietnamese referee issued only a verbal warning. Regional football discipline norms are now in play.

What I am carrying into next week: SEA's accountability stories and its AI-labor stories are converging. Workforce-with-dignity is no longer separable from the AI-and-capital question. Where does that conversation start in your organisation?
