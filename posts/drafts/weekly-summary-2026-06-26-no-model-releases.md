Ten tech-only stories from Jun 22-26, with the two frontier model launches (GPT-5.6, Mythos 5) deliberately set aside. What's left is the substrate: cost, defense, and dependency. The chip, cyber, and enterprise-pricing layers all moved this week — and they tell a different week-shape.

1. OpenAI and Broadcom unveiled Jalapeño Tuesday — OpenAI's first custom inference chip, ~50% cheaper than typical AI GPUs. Nine-month tape-out (fastest ASIC of this class ever). GW-scale via Microsoft starts end-2026, runs through 2029.

2. Qualcomm bought Modular for $3.92B in stock. Modular's software runs AI models across any chip — the cleanest CUDA-alternative play built so far. Qualcomm is now in the AI infrastructure fight, not just at the edge.

3. OpenAI expanded Daybreak on Monday — releasing GPT-5.5-Cyber (security-specialized model), Codex Security updates, and Patch the Planet (open-source maintainer initiative with Trail of Bits). Focus shifted from finding vulnerabilities to patching them at scale.

4. Anthropic told the Senate Banking Committee that Alibaba/Qwen-linked actors ran ~25,000 fake accounts for 28.8M Claude exchanges between April 22 and June 5 — targeting agentic reasoning, software engineering, long-horizon tasks. Congress preparing sanctions.

5. NVIDIA at ISC unveiled the BioNeMo Agent Toolkit, Halos for Robotics (first full-stack physical-AI safety system), and 35 new European AI supercomputers in development. The sovereign-infra count keeps building.

6. CISA issued an emergency directive on Check Point VPN zero-day CVE-2026-50751 — actively exploited by ransomware via unauthenticated IKEv1 remote access. Patch immediately. The perimeter attack surface keeps widening.

7. Enterprise AI buyers are tired of token-volume pitches and rotating to measured efficiency — CNBC's Friday read. The frontier-spend narrative hit a cost-discipline wall. Same wall Jalapeño's 50% cut is built to climb.

8. Microsoft 365 Copilot Business goes permanent July 1 — Standard with Copilot $23.50, Premium $32 per user/month. Standalone Copilot Business cut from $30 to $21 list ($18 with annual promo). Discount era closing.

9. Anthropic shipped Trusted Devices for Claude Code admins, Slack tagging for Enterprise, custom-role admin permissions, higher API rate limits, and consolidated tiers (Start/Build/Scale). Frontier-model usage finally gets SSO/RBAC primitives.

10. A severe OpenSSL vulnerability allows RCE via crafted PKCS7 or S/MIME messages. Patch sweeps on any system handling signed external content are non-negotiable — touches every TLS-using app in the estate.

Carrying into next week: with the model layer set aside, the week's story is cost-down, defense-up, dependency-real. Models lens ran thin — that's what excluding GPT-5.6 and Mythos 5 does to the shape. The substrate is what moved.
