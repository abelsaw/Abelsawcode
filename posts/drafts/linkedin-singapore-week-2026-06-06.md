## Option A — Measured (96 words)

Singapore's week, read through an employer's lens, had one clear subject: people.

From July 1, the Local Qualifying Salary rises to S$1,800, up from S$1,600. It resets how foreign-worker quotas are counted, and SMEs that miss the transition risk losing headcount. The time to review renewals is now, not July.

Alongside it, MediSave coverage extended to fertility preservation for patients facing treatments like chemotherapy. And a senior minister stepped back from office for his family.

Wages, benefits, the cost of demanding roles. Different headlines, one theme.

Policy here keeps moving. The planning shouldn't wait.
