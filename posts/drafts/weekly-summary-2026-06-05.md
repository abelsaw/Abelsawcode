Ten stories from May 30 to June 5, ranked by how loudly the catalog cited them. Capital markets did the talking this week. Microsoft Build reset the agent runtime conversation. Washington narrowed its AI order. The HR lens stayed quiet — which is its own signal.

1. Alphabet upsized its equity offering to about $85 billion for AI capex, with Berkshire taking $10 billion privately. The model layer is financing growth at a pace usually reserved for crises.

2. Microsoft Build set Windows up as an OS for agents — Autopilots, Microsoft Scout, Microsoft IQ — plus MAI-Code-1-Flash to reduce OpenAI dependence. Build-vs-buy now resolves as "both, but the OS is Microsoft's."

3. Trump signed a narrowed AI executive order on Tuesday: voluntary 30-day government access to frontier models, plus a cybersecurity clearinghouse. The mandatory version got pulled May 21. Federal compliance has a voluntary lane now.

4. The FT reported Friday that Meta is exploring tens of billions in new equity for AI. Meta called it "pure speculation." Stock dropped 6.6%. If it lands, that's two mega-tech raises in one week.

5. OpenAI launched Codex Enterprise with six vertical plug-ins — data, sales, design, creative, investing, banking — plus a Sites feature with Wix, Replit, Figma, Lovable, Base44, Emergent. Six white-collar fronts in one keynote.

6. Nvidia entered the PC chip market with RTX Spark and MediaTek, debuting in Windows laptops from Microsoft, Dell, HP, Asus, Lenovo and MSI. The endpoint is now an AI surface.

7. HPE reported $10.7B Q2 revenue (+40%), $2.1B in new AI server orders, $5.9B backlog, raised guidance. Stock +23% intraday. The AI infrastructure trade is broadening past Nvidia.

8. Morgan Stanley will open its $1.2 trillion workplace wealth platform — ShareWorks and Equity Edge — to corporate clients' AI agents. Major bank platforms going agent-accessible is now signal, not experiment.

9. CrowdStrike posted record net new ARR of $256M (+32%), raised guidance, announced a 4-for-1 split, and launched Project QuiltWorks — a coalition with OpenAI and Anthropic on frontier AI risk. AI security is consolidating around the labs you already buy.

10. Anthropic confidentially filed for an IPO on Sunday, days after the $965B Series H. The IPO calendar for foundation labs is no longer hypothetical. Public-market discipline starts shaping pricing earlier than expected.

Nine of those ten sit on the Business Strategy or IT side of my desk. Zero major HR-specific stories cleared the bar this week. That's a tell. The capital and infrastructure layers are moving faster than the labor side. As a CTO whose remit spans strategy, HR and IT, that gap is what I'm carrying into next week.
