## Selection basis (why this pair now)

most-mentioned company of the month. Trigger: May 20, 2026 Q1 FY27
earnings call — record $81.6B revenue (+85% YoY), $5.22T market cap (May
24), Jensen Huang's "Agentic AI has arrived" / "Demand has gone
parabolic." AMD is the direct architectural rival in the same market,
and its own Q1 2026 print (May 5/6) was the biggest semis story two
weeks earlier — $10.3B revenue (+38%), $762B market cap, +120% YTD.

## Candidate angles (ranked)

1. **"Nvidia owns the spend. AMD owns the upside."** — Picked. The data tension is real: Nvidia is the dollars story; AMD is the percentage-gain story. AMD stock +120% YTD even though Nvidia did 8x its revenue. The argument: where can share / valuation actually go from 85% and $5.22T? AMD has room. (Used for all 3 posts, three hooks.)
2. "Compute is the new oil. Both win for the rest of the decade." Too soft — doesn't pick a side.
3. "AMD's only hope is inference." Too narrow — misses the Meta 6 GW deal scale.
4. "Nvidia is uninvestable above $5T." Too provocative, not professional voice.
5. "TSMC wins either way." True but it's a third-party angle, not a comparison of these two.
