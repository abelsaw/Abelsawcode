## Draft (96 words)

Anthropic just published something more important than any model release.

In "When AI Builds Itself," it reports that Claude now writes over 80% of the code merged into its own codebase. Its engineers ship roughly 8x more per day than in 2024. AI is now accelerating AI.

Their worry isn't raw power. It's recursive self-improvement: systems improving their own successors faster than humans can govern.

So they propose a verifiable, coordinated pause. Brakes designed before the threshold, not after.

The lesson for any board: build the guardrails before the curve goes vertical, not once it has.
