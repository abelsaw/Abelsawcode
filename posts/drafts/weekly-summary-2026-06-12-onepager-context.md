## Story 1 — SpaceX IPO (rank 1, citation count 10+)

### What landed this week
- **Jun 12 (Fri):** Began trading on Nasdaq under ticker **SPCX**. Priced at **$135**, closed at **$160.95** (+19%). About **$75B raised** — the largest IPO in history. First-day market cap **above $2T**, briefly above $2.25T per BusinessToday's live coverage.
- **Jun 11 (Thu):** Pricing date.
- **Jun 4 (the prior Thu):** Roadshow launched at a compressed cadence — went straight to a fixed $135 price, bypassing the typical range process.
- **Goldman Sachs is lead-left**, beating Morgan Stanley, JPMorgan, Citigroup, Bank of America and UBS.

### Year-to-date arc
- **Jan-Feb 2026:** Financial Times exclusives surface the planned 2026 IPO targeting a **$1.5T valuation**.
- **Q1 2026:** **Starlink crosses 5M subscribers** (February), then 10M shortly after. Starlink generated **$11.4B revenue in 2025** — 61% of SpaceX's total — and posted **$4.4B operating profit**, the only consistently GAAP-profitable segment. ARPU fell 18% to ~$81/month.
- **Apr-May 2026:** Pre-IPO valuation guidance climbs to ~$1.77T-$1.78T per the S-1 filing. Goldman Sachs research forecast: SpaceX AI division revenue rising from **$3.2B (2025) → $322B (2030)** — a 100× bull case.
- **Jun 3 2026:** Roadshow targets fixed $135 price.
- **Jun 12 2026:** Listed. **First-day cap above $2T** — instantly the seventh-largest US company by market cap. The deal raised up to **~$86B**.

### CTO read
The space-and-compute bet now has a public-market price. **Every internal capex assumption tied to Starlink, Starshield or SpaceX-adjacent infrastructure now re-anchors to a daily SPCX equity curve.** It also resets the comparable for what an AI-adjacent mega-listing looks like — the path OpenAI, Anthropic and xAI will reference.

## Story 3 — Anthropic Claude Fable 5 (rank 3, citation count 8+)

### What landed this week
- **Jun 9 (Tue):** Anthropic released **Claude Fable 5** — its first generally available **Mythos-class** model, a new tier above Opus. Same-day release of **Claude Mythos 5** for restricted trusted-access use.
- **Pricing:** **$10 input / $50 output per million tokens** on the Anthropic API.
- **Context window:** **1M tokens** input, up to **128k tokens** output.
- **Distribution:** Claude API, Claude Platform on AWS, Amazon Bedrock, Google Vertex AI, **Microsoft Foundry** — a five-cloud day-one footprint.
- **Bundle:** **Free** on Pro, Max, Team and seat-based Enterprise plans through **June 22**. Metered after that at the same $10/$50 token rates.
- **Capability flag:** Mythos 5 (preview) demonstrated the ability to autonomously discover and chain zero-day exploits across major operating systems and browsers during a restricted preview — flagged in Anthropic's release notes.

### Year-to-date arc
- **Feb 2026:** **Claude Opus 4.6** ships — 1M-token context (beta), adaptive thinking mode, Agent Teams.
- **Mar-Apr 2026:** Opus 4.7 series with managed-agents infra ships (covered in the May 22 weekly summary).
- **May 28 2026:** **Claude Opus 4.8** ships with dynamic workflow tool (per the May 29 weekly summary).
- **May 28 2026:** Anthropic closes **Series H at $65B raise / $965B post-money valuation** — surpasses OpenAI's then-$852B mark as the most valuable AI startup.
- **Jun 1 2026:** Anthropic **confidentially files for IPO** (carried in last week's summary at rank 10).
- **Jun 9 2026:** **Fable 5 GA + Mythos 5 trusted-access** release.

### CTO read
A new tier above Opus is now generally available across every major hyperscaler at one consistent price. **The frontier tier has a public price tag for procurement** — and it's lower than Mythos Preview was. Combined with the IPO-track filing two weeks earlier, Anthropic in 2026 has moved from "research lab adjacent to AWS" to a **publicly distributed, IPO-track, multi-cloud frontier vendor.**

## Story 5 — OpenAI confidential S-1 (rank 5, citation count 7+)

### What landed this week
- **Jun 8 (Mon):** OpenAI confirmed it had submitted a **confidential S-1** to the SEC. Company statement: *"We recently submitted a confidential S-1. We expect it to leak so we're just announcing it."*
- **Caveat from OpenAI:** It "may be a while" before going public — they emphasized this preserves optionality rather than committing to a date.
- **Most-cited listing window:** **September-November 2026** (~10 forecasts converge here), though nothing is locked in.

### Year-to-date arc
- **Jan 2026:** WSJ reports OpenAI laying groundwork for a Q4 2026 public listing.
- **Jan 2026:** CFO Sarah Friar confirms annualized revenue passed **$20B** (from $6B in 2024).
- **Late Feb 2026:** Annualized revenue run rate **surpasses $25B**.
- **Mar 31 2026:** Closes **$122B funding round** at **$852B post-money valuation** — largest private financing in Silicon Valley history. Amazon leads with $50B (of which $35B is contingent on IPO by end-2028 or AGI), Nvidia and SoftBank each commit $30B.
- **Q1-Q2 2026:** **Stargate Project** spending pledge revised down from $1.4T to $600B per reporting (May 19). OpenAI shifts from "build it all" to a hybrid build-and-rent model. Cash burn projected ~$27B in 2026 (from ~$8B in 2025).
- **May 2026:** Reports of a possible confidential S-1 filing in May 20-22 window (per Tech Insider forecast tracking).
- **Jun 8 2026:** OpenAI **publicly confirms** the confidential submission.

### CTO read
Public-market discipline starts shaping behavior the moment the filing exists — **not when the listing happens.** Expect changes in OpenAI's pricing cadence, partnership terms, model release pacing, and hiring posture over the next two quarters. Plus: with Anthropic confidentially filed (Jun 1) and OpenAI confirmed (Jun 8) **inside the same 7-day window**, the two-horse frontier race now has parallel IPO calendars.

## Story 7 — Palantir's Karp on CNBC (rank 7, citation count 6+)

### What landed this week
- **Jun 10 (Tue):** Karp told CNBC's Sara Eisen — in an on-camera interview:
  - *"It's not just the man and woman on the street that is unhappy with the frontier labs, it's in private, every single enterprise we deal with."*
  - The labs are doing **"tokenmaxxing"** — burning tokens to signal productivity rather than producing measurable business outcomes.
  - **"Most of Anthropic's publicly discussed projects are running on Palantir."**
  - Enterprise customers want **software that operates inside existing business systems** and produces measurable results.

### Year-to-date arc — why this lands hard
- **Q4 2025 (reported Feb 2026):** Palantir US government revenue grew **66% YoY** to $570M in Q4. Closed a record **$4.262B in total contract value, up 138% YoY**.
- **May 4 2026 (Q1 2026 earnings):**
  - **Total revenue $1.63B, +85% YoY** — Palantir's highest-ever YoY growth rate.
  - **US commercial revenue $595M, +133% YoY** (and +18% QoQ). Karp's guide: that figure could accelerate to **+120% by year's end**.
  - **Rule of 40 score: 145%** — putting Palantir in the same tier as **NVIDIA, Micron and SK hynix** by Karp's framing.
  - Full-year revenue guide raised to **+71% growth**.
  - Adjusted income-from-operations guide raised to $4.44-$4.45B; adjusted FCF $4.2-$4.4B.
- **Throughout 2026:** Palantir AIP (Artificial Intelligence Platform) positioned as **the integration layer between enterprises and the models** — not a model competitor, but the connective tissue. Karp's repeated public posture: enterprises don't want models, they want outcomes inside their existing systems.
- **Jun 10 2026:** The "tokenmaxxing" frame and the "every single enterprise is unhappy" line land in the same week SpaceX prices, OpenAI files, and Anthropic ships Fable 5. **Karp is taking the integrator's seat at the IPO table.**

### CTO read
The supply side just got publicly graded by a buyer that talks like one. **Procurement leverage shifts to integrators that operate inside the system of record, not labs that operate inside the model.** Karp's "running on Palantir" claim about Anthropic projects is a tell: if true, the frontier labs need the integrator more than the integrator needs any one lab. That changes vendor-management posture for 2026 H2.
