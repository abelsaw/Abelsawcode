Ten stories from Jun 8-12. The week the AI IPO calendar went from hypothetical to actual. SpaceX listed above $2T. OpenAI confirmed a confidential filing. Apple rebuilt Siri on Gemini. Anthropic shipped its first Mythos-class model. Oracle and Adobe printed record AI revenue. Palantir's Karp said the buyer's verdict out loud. Ranked by how loudly they were cited.

1. SpaceX listed Friday on Nasdaq as SPCX. A $135 offer closed at $160.95 (+19%); ~$75B raised, the largest IPO in history; first-day cap above $2T. The space-and-compute bet now has a public-market price.

2. Apple's WWDC keynote Monday rebuilt Siri on a 1.2-trillion-parameter Google Gemini model. iOS 27 and macOS Golden Gate ship later this year. The default consumer AI in a billion devices is now Gemini under the hood.

3. Anthropic released Claude Fable 5 on Tuesday — its first generally available Mythos-class model — at $10 / $50 per million tokens. Free on Pro, Max, Team and Enterprise through June 22. The frontier tier now has a public price tag.

4. Oracle Q4 FY26: record $19.2B revenue (+21%), cloud $9.9B (+47%), $638B RPO with $67B of new AI infra contracts in-quarter. The AI compute trade is broadening past the obvious names.

5. OpenAI confirmed Monday it had confidentially filed an S-1. "We expect it to leak so we're just announcing it." A late-2026 listing window is now legible.

6. Adobe Q2 FY26: record $6.62B revenue (+13%); AI-first ARR crossed $500M, tripled year over year. AI revenue at the application layer is no longer a slide footnote.

7. Palantir's Karp told CNBC Tuesday "every single enterprise we deal with" is unhappy with the frontier labs, called out "tokenmaxxing," and said most of Anthropic's public projects "run on Palantir." The supply side just got publicly graded by a buyer.

8. CrowdStrike's 2026 Technology Threat Landscape Report named tech the world's most-targeted industry and AI models, training data and ML infra the prime IP targets. China-linked groups: 58% of state-sponsored attacks. Weights belong on the crown-jewels tier.

9. GitLab restructured for "the agentic AI era": ~350 roles affected, three management layers flattened, exits in 22 countries — while revenue grew 23% YoY. Growth and headcount are decoupling at agentic-native firms.

10. Amdocs disclosed a 2,900-role reorganization Tuesday, AI in the rationale. Second straight week with AI-coded productivity showing up in workforce notices alongside healthy top-line.

What I am carrying into next week: this is the first week the buyer's verdict and the seller's IPO landed in the same cycle. Karp's "unhappy" lands harder when SpaceX trades above $2T the same Friday. Capacity is being financed publicly; outcomes have not caught up. For a CTO whose remit spans strategy, HR and IT, that gap is where the next twelve months play out.
