Five trading days. Four stories. One thread.

SpaceX listed at over $2 trillion — $2.2T by Friday's after-hours. OpenAI confirmed a confidential S-1 and the $1T-listing chatter started the same week. Anthropic shipped its first Mythos-class model at a public price — and at 80.3% on SWE-Bench Pro, 11 points clear of the next-best model. Palantir's Alex Karp went on CNBC and told the labs the buyer is unhappy.

Capital. Capability. Distribution. Demand verdict.

All in the same news cycle.

2026 is the year AI moved from a balance-sheet line to a publicly priced asset — and this week the calendar caught up to the revenue line. The supply side is being financed at a pace no capex roadmap was built for. The buyer's verdict has not caught up to the seller's valuation.

Karp's "every single enterprise is unhappy" lands harder when SpaceX trades above $2T the same Friday — and when, in the same interview, he also calls Anthropic the "leading frontier model company." The verdict is sharper than the headline: the labs matter; the integrators win the enterprise.

For a CTO whose remit spans strategy, HR and IT, the next twelve months are about closing that gap — at procurement, at org design, and at the integration layer that decides whether the model becomes an outcome or just a token bill.

What are you carrying into next week?
