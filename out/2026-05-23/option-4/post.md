# Option 4 — SpaceX is selling the multiplanetary vision to public markets

"Starship is the key to making life multiplanetary." Elon Musk's line in the SpaceX S-1 is not engineering. It is positioning.

SpaceX is going public as a single integrated bet: reusable rockets, AI infrastructure investments, and a multiplanetary horizon — financed by public markets for the first time.

In the next 12 months, capital markets will be asked a new question: does an AI compute backbone belong on the same balance sheet as orbital launch capacity? Anthropic's $1.25B/month commitment suggests yes.

Are public investors underwriting space, or are they underwriting compute disguised as space?
