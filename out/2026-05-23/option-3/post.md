# Option 3 — SpaceX's S-1 is the most ambitious AI bet ever filed

This is not a space IPO. SpaceX filed its S-1 on May 20 targeting a $1.75T valuation under ticker SPCX.

Buried in the filing: a $1.25B-per-month compute commitment from Anthropic through May 2029, a $60B option on Cursor, and xAI now an integral pillar.

Compute is now a balance-sheet asset class. Over the next 12 months, every CFO running an AI roadmap will be benchmarked against multi-year compute commitments, not annual cloud spend.

Is your AI budget still framed as opex, or are you starting to model it like capex?
