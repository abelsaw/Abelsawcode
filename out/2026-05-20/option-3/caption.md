# Option 3 — Google just redrew the search box — after 25 years

**Source:** https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/search-io-2026/

---

Google described its Search redesign at I/O 2026 as the biggest change to the search entry point in over 25 years. That is not marketing language. It is an accurate description of what actually changed.

Gemini 3.5 Flash is now the default model for AI Mode — globally, for everyone, starting yesterday. The search box itself has been redesigned. And users can now create and manage multiple AI agents directly inside Search, without leaving the interface.

That last capability is the one that rewrites the business model.

Search has always been about finding information. Adding persistent agents inside the search interface changes the contract to accomplishing tasks. Book a flight, monitor a price, draft an email — from the same box you've used to search for 25 years. The shift from 'find information' to 'do the thing' has enormous implications for how attention, time, and advertising revenue flow through the product.

The default model change is not a feature announcement either. It is a distribution event. Every person who opens Google now interacts with Gemini whether or not they opted in. No standalone AI product — not ChatGPT, not Claude.ai — has comparable passive reach. Google is using the moat it already holds to make the AI transition frictionless at a scale its competitors cannot match.

SEO assumptions built before yesterday are already partially wrong. What part of your search strategy needs to change first?
