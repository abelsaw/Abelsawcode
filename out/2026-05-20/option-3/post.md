KPMG just put Claude in front of 276,000 employees and inside the platform clients pay them to use.

On May 19, KPMG and Anthropic signed a global alliance and launched Digital Gateway Powered by Claude. Every KPMG professional gets Claude. More importantly, Cowork and Managed Agents are embedded inside Digital Gateway, the Azure-based platform KPMG uses for tax and legal delivery, with the rest of advisory to follow by September. Anthropic also named KPMG a preferred private-equity consulting partner, with co-built products planned for PE portfolio companies.

The contrarian read: the Big Four are not buyers of AI. They are becoming the distribution layer for it. When KPMG agents write your tax memo, the model behind it is a procurement decision your firm did not make.

Three things to plan around in the next 6 to 12 months:

1. Audit and tax workpapers shift from human-drafted to model-drafted with human review. Pricing pressure follows.
2. PE portfolios get AI uplift programs branded by their accounting firm, not their CTO.
3. Microsoft Azure becomes the default enterprise AI substrate by gravity, not by RFP.
