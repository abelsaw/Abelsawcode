# Option 1 — Anthropic just landed the AI world's most wanted hire

**Source:** https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/19/anthropic-hires-openai-cofounder-andrej-karpathy-former-tesla-ai-lead.html

---

Andrej Karpathy just joined Anthropic to lead pretraining research. That sentence should stop anyone who follows AI cold.

Karpathy is not a product executive or a fundraising face. He is one of the small number of people alive who has shaped the architecture and training dynamics of frontier models at multiple institutions — OpenAI as co-founder, Tesla Autopilot as director of AI, and now Anthropic. His mandate is specific: build a team and use Claude itself to accelerate pretraining research.

That last part is the real story.

Using a frontier model as a tool to improve the process that builds the next frontier model is a recursive loop that every major lab is trying to operationalize. Karpathy doing it at Anthropic — with the standing to recruit the researchers who can actually execute — is a structural bet, not a PR move.

Most coverage will focus on the name. The right question is what it signals about where the pretraining work is happening and who believes Anthropic's trajectory justifies the move.

Talent at the model-foundation layer leads capability by 12-18 months. Labs are won or lost at pretraining. Karpathy just voted with his career.
