Google I/O 2026 was not a model release. It was a re-platforming of Google around Gemini.

In a two-hour keynote at Shoreline, Sundar Pichai made Gemini 3.5 Flash the default model behind Search, shipped Gemini Omni for real-time video reasoning, and introduced Spark, an agentic assistant that runs across Workspace, Android and Chrome. Antigravity 2.0 turned Google's coding tool into an agent-first IDE.

The strategic read: Google is no longer protecting Search from AI. It is rebuilding Search as AI, with Gemini Omni and Spark as the new ad surface. That is a different bet than the OpenAI one. OpenAI sells access to a model. Google is welding the model into every product 2 billion people already open.

For the next 6 to 12 months, three things to plan around:

1. Default model assumptions in your stack will break. Gemini 3.5 Flash latency changes what is affordable in production.
2. Search referral traffic continues to compress. Build for citations inside answers, not blue links.
3. Workspace becomes an agent runtime. Plan for shared context with Gemini, not screenshots.

Which Gemini surface forces the biggest change to your roadmap this quarter — Search, Workspace, or Antigravity?
