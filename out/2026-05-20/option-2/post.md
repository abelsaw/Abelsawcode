# Option 2 — OpenAI Plants Its First Lab Outside The US

OpenAI just signed its first applied AI lab outside the United States. It is in Singapore, not London or Paris.

At ATxSummit on May 20, OpenAI committed S$300 million (roughly US$234 million) under an MoU with Singapore's Ministry of Digital Development and Information. The lab will hire more than 200 technical staff and focus on three things: deploying frontier AI inside large organisations, training local talent, and broadening access across the economy. Healthcare, finance, public services and education are named as initial workstreams.

The angle that matters: Singapore is becoming the operating system for enterprise AI in Asia. Government as anchor customer, regulator and co-investor in one. That is a model Brussels, Riyadh and Abu Dhabi will copy.

For leaders running APAC, three takeaways:

1. The centre of gravity for enterprise AI hiring just moved. Expect a talent squeeze in Singapore by Q3.
2. Sovereign AI is no longer a slide. It is procurement language.
3. If you sell to APAC enterprises, your reference architecture needs a Singapore-compliant data path.

Where does this lab change your APAC AI plan — talent, data residency, or go-to-market?
